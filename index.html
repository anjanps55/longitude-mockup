Condition Briefing
Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis (FSGS)
Diagnosed Feb 2026 · Updated today
🧬
Genetic Testing May Change Your Treatment
8-35% of FSGS cases have a genetic cause. Genetic FSGS generally does not respond to steroids. In one study, 50% of patients with genetic variants received unnecessary immunosuppression.
Source: Kidney International, 2024 · APOL1 Research Consortium
Key Facts
What You Should Know
  • FSGS accounts for 40% of adult nephrotic syndrome
  • Over 50 monogenic causes of steroid-resistant FSGS identified
  • Genetic testing can prevent months of unnecessary treatment
  • APOL1 high-risk variants found in ~14% of African Americans
  • Genetic FSGS has different transplant recurrence risk
Questions for Your Nephrologist
Priority 1
"Should I get genetic testing before starting immunosuppressive therapy?"
8-35% of FSGS is genetic and steroid-resistant. Testing can prevent ineffective treatment. (Kidney International, 2024)
Priority 2
"What specific genetic panel should be ordered for FSGS?"
Exome sequencing found diagnostic variants in 29% of adults with FSGS. (JASN, 2023)
Priority 3
"If I do have genetic FSGS, what are the treatment alternatives?"
Genetic FSGS requires targeted management based on the specific mutation. (Nature Reviews Nephrology, 2024)
Also Ask
"Should my family members be tested for hereditary implications?"
Genetic counseling recommended for all confirmed genetic FSGS cases. (KDIGO, 2025)
